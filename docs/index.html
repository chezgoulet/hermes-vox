Hermes Vox

a particle-being you talk to.

An open-source voice client for the Hermes agent. On-device speech, hands-free, and the being that answers you.

A voice client for the Hermes agent. Speak; the being — a luminous particle-swarm — listens, thinks, and answers. On-device (Whisper STT, Silero VAD, Piper TTS), no button, barge-in.

Bring your own Hermes gateway. Vox connects to your gateway (local, self-hosted, or one you run) with an endpoint + API key you enter in-app. Not a cloud service. Your audio and key stay with you.

Two modes: Realtime and Enhanced Realtime (alpha). Early days — rapid development.
