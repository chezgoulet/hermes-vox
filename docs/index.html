Hermes Vox

a particle-being you talk to.

An open-source voice client for the Hermes agent — on-device speech, hands-free, and the being that answers you.

What it is

Hermes Vox turns your phone into a place where the Hermes agent is present and speaks. On-device speech processing (Whisper STT, Silero VAD, Piper TTS via sherpa-onnx) keeps your voice on the phone; the being — a luminous particle-swarm — is the agent made visible. Barge-in, hands-free, no button.

Two voice modesRealtime (hands-free) + Enhanced Realtime (alpha) — adds an on-device Gemma expression layer, experimental.
Your gatewayYou bring your own Hermes gateway. Vox is a client — your data stays with you, not a cloud service.
The beingA 20-archetype particle-swarm. Breathing, listening, thinking, speaking — the agent as light.
Bring your own Hermes gateway. Hermes Vox connects to your Hermes gateway (local, self-hosted, or one you run) with an endpoint + API key you enter in-app. It is not a cloud service from us. Your audio and key stay with you and your gateway.
