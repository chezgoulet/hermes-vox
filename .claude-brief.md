## What must be preserved (the reason this is hard)

The Go state machine carries four specific fixes. Do not lose them:

1. the wall-clock deadline (H1),
2. the `/compress` chain (H2) — it must ride the same server-side response chain,
3. a leak fix (M1),
4. a data race fix between the SSE writer and the poller.

`voice/stream_contract_test.go` and `voice/entity_race_test.go` define that
behaviour. They are the spec.
